Devices and fabrication

  1. 85 to 88 nmAZO films, fit MSE below 7

    Aluminum-doped zinc oxide by ALD Sep 2026 to present

    An ITO replacement grown on a Cambridge S200 ALD tool: a 200 °C DEZ/TMA/H₂O doping series at 99:1, 49:1, and 19:1 ZnO:Al cycle ratios. Spectroscopic ellipsometry fits in WVASE give thickness, refractive index, and band edge for each film. The band edge shifts 0.2 eV (3.35 to 3.58 eV) with doping, with free-carrier absorption at 19:1. At the Quattrone Nanofabrication Facility.

Experience

  1. Quattrone Nanofabrication Facility, Penn

    Graduate Student Fellow

    Sep 2026 to present

    I am developing an atomic layer deposition (ALD) process for aluminum-doped zinc oxide (AZO) as a replacement for ITO. I grow ZnO:Al films on a Cambridge S200 ALD tool, fit each film's optical response with spectroscopic ellipsometry in WVASE, and use XRD and my own Python analysis to tie doping level to film properties.

    • 200 °C doping series from DEZ, TMA, and H₂O at 99:1, 49:1, and 19:1 ZnO:Al cycle ratios.
    • Ellipsometry fits give 85 to 88 nm film thickness at fit MSE below 7.
    • 0.2 eV Burstein-Moss band-edge shift (3.35 to 3.58 eV) and free-carrier absorption at 19:1, consistent with literature.

About

I am an MSE Electrical Engineering student at the University of Pennsylvania, graduating May 2027. My work sits between analog IC design and the fab. I design circuits in Cadence (a 45 nm TIA, an SRAM array) and I also grow and characterize the films those circuits depend on. At Penn's Quattrone Nanofabrication Facility I am developing an ALD process for aluminum-doped zinc oxide as a replacement for ITO. Before Penn I did a B.E. in Electronics and Communication at Thapar in India and spent a summer building motor driver hardware and firmware for warehouse robots.

Long term, I want to work on analog and mixed-signal silicon in the US and then build a semiconductor manufacturing business in India.
